Privacy Policy

Who we are

OpenWorld Travel is a Kenya-based travel planning business. For questions about personal information or to exercise your privacy rights, contact hello@openworldtravel.tours.

Information we collect

When preparing an enquiry, you may provide your name, email, country of residence, travel dates, group size, budget and preferences. Providing this information is voluntary, but without your name, email and essential trip details we may be unable to respond or prepare a quotation. Please do not include passport numbers, payment card details, medical information or other sensitive information in this form.

How we use information

We use enquiry information to respond to your request, prepare itineraries and quotations, arrange agreed services, maintain necessary business records and protect our website. We process information as needed to take steps you request before a contract, fulfil a contract, meet legal obligations or pursue legitimate security and business interests where appropriate. Separate consent will be requested where required.

Website and email providers

Vercel hosts the website. Google Fonts and Unsplash provide external fonts and images; the exchange-rate service provides currency information. These services may receive technical information such as IP addresses and browser details. When enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile checks for automated abuse and Resend delivers enquiry and acknowledgement emails. Our business email provider receives enquiries. If you use the manual email option, your own email application handles transmission; downloaded summaries remain on your device.

Sharing and international processing

Where necessary to arrange a requested journey, relevant details may be shared with accommodation, transport and other travel suppliers. Information may also be disclosed when legally required. Providers may process information outside Kenya; international transfers must be subject to a lawful transfer mechanism and appropriate safeguards as required by applicable law.

Retention and security

Enquiries and booking correspondence should be retained only as long as needed for the stated purposes, legal obligations and legitimate dispute handling. Website security measures include server-side validation and restricted browser permissions; automated sending uses an anti-abuse check when enabled. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

Under applicable Kenyan data protection law, you may request information about processing, access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability where applicable, object to processing and withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Contact us by email. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner at www.odpc.go.ke.

Changes and contact

This notice may be updated as our services change. Contact hello@openworldtravel.tours for privacy questions.
